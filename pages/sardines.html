Rödel’s sardines

The secret
of perfection.

A small fishing boat sets out each morning to a carefully selected place, returns the very same day, and the fish are prepared while still fresh.

{{fig:hands-open-tins}}
  1. 01

    The season

    Our fishing season runs only from May to October, when sardines are at their nutritional best and naturally richest in healthy fats. We respect their breeding season, and nature’s timeless cycle of renewal.

  2. 02

    The waters

    Our sardines are caught exclusively in protected waters off the coast of Brittany: the Bay of Concarneau, the waters around the Glénan archipelago and Cape Trévignon.

  3. 03

    The cannery

    Our cannery is in Concarneau, known as the sardine capital. Every August the town hosts the Fête des Filets Bleus, the Festival of the Blue Nets.

  4. 04

    The fishermen

    Local fishermen use the traditional purse-seine method known as bolinche. We work only with nine fishing families from Concarneau.

  5. 05

    The same day

    Sardines caught in the morning are packed into tins that very same day. They are never frozen: we use only fresh fish.

  6. 06

    By hand

    Every sardine is cleaned by hand and carefully arranged in the tin. Before packing, the sardines are fried for thirty seconds to seal the surface and preserve the firmness of the flesh.

  7. 07

    The ingredients

    We use only first-class ingredients, including olive oil, salt and lemons sourced from carefully selected farms.

  8. 08

    Never a compromise

    The quality of our product will always be more important than its cost. We never compromise.

On every lid

Rödel indicates on each lid when, by whom, and where the fish were caught.

{{fig:concarneau}}

Furthermore, access to the cannery is almost as restricted as admission to Eton: 100% of the candidates must be Breton, that is to say wild, right size and optimal fat.

Concerning morality, the Rödel Recipes and Instructions Book is unequivocal: “Frozen and deep-frozen sardines, whatever their apparent quality and presentation, will be systematically turned away. This rule tolerates no exception.”

Only sardines freshly recruited during the five months of the fishing season will be allowed to pass the cannery barrier. Endowed with such a pedigree, the Rödel sardine becomes the indispensable companion of chosen moments.
