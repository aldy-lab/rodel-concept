01 — The same process since 1824

Caught in the morning.
In the tin the same day.

Nine fishing families of Concarneau bring in the catch with the traditional bolinche, from the Bay of Concarneau, the Glénan archipelago and Cape Trévignon. Every sardine is cleaned by hand, fried for thirty seconds to seal it, and laid in the tin. Never frozen.

02 — Millésime

The finest catch
of a given year.

A designation reserved for the finest catch of a given year, harvested at optimal maturity for exceptional richness and remarkable depth of flavour. Rödel marks on each lid when, by whom and where the fish were caught.

    04 — Since 1824

    The oldest cannery brand
    in the world.

    In 1820 the French Admiralty asked Nicolas Appert, the inventor of preserving, to build a cannery in Bordeaux. He entrusted it to his most accomplished disciple, Charles Rödel.

    1. 1804Nicolas Appert opens the first cannery, in Massy.
    2. 1824Rödel & Fils Frères is founded in Bordeaux.
    3. 1872A Rödel factory opens in Concarneau.
    4. c. 1880The emblem: a globe paired with a beehive.
    5. 2025Millésime 2025, from the finest catch of the year.
