01 — The method

Packed by hand.
Unchanged since 1824.

Each sardine is laid into its tin by hand, in Brittany, by the method the house has kept for two centuries. The fish are responsibly sourced, and the Millésime is prepared on the day of the catch.

02 — Millésime

Dated by the catch,
like wine by its harvest.

Each year the house selects the finest catch and marks the tin with its year. The sardines are taken at optimal maturity, for richness and depth of flavour, and laid down like a cellar wine: the Classic keeps its quality for up to ten years.

04 — Two centuries

The oldest sardine house
in the world.

Rödel & Fils Frères. One family name, one process, and recipes kept in the house catalogues since the nineteenth century.

  1. 1824The house is founded. Its preserving method has not changed since.
  2. c. 1905Chica Pica: a spice blend from a Venezuelan friend of the house.
  3. 1926A single-serving recipe, later recovered from an original catalogue.
  4. 2025Millésime 2025: hand-boned, from the finest catch of the year.
