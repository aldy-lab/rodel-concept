Millésime 2025

Sardines à l’huile d’olive vierge extra · Millésime 2025

The cornerstone of the art of preservation.

This product formed the very cornerstone of the art of preservation and served as the benchmark for all other canneries. Created by Charles Rödel’s son, it followed a precise and unmistakable method, ensuring a taste and quality that may be preserved, and savoured, even after ten years.

Rödel Vintage Sardines harmoniously balance the savory flavor of high-quality fish resourcefully sourced from prime sardine populations with the subtlety of premium olive oil. The sardines embody a strong aroma, carrying whispers of the pristine waters they were naturally bred in.

05Recipes of the house

Recipes of the house · 115 g

Chica Pica

Sardines Chica Pica à l’huile d’olive vierge extra & aux aromates

A secret spice blend, and a registered trademark of the house.

Chica Pica, “wild girl”: a secret spice blend created for Rödel in the early 20th century by a Venezuelan friend. A registered trademark and enduring culinary signature of the House.

These gourmet sardines are imbued with a delightful aroma that is characteristic of high-quality seafood. The sardines are infused with the original Chica Pica herb blend. The infusion of a blend of unique aromates, including carrot, pickle, chili, white pepper, clove, and laurel, collectively contribute to the distinctive aroma of the product. The taste profile is rich and full-flavored, with a slightly salty and smoky taste that adds depth and complexity.

Recipes of the house · 115 g

Citron

Sardines à l’huile d’olive vierge extra au citron

Extra virgin olive oil and a whole sun-ripened lemon.

Combination that lasts for centuries. Fish and lemon form a classic harmony, much like love and marriage in a timeless jazz refrain. Combined with extra virgin olive oil and a sun-ripened whole lemon, they create an unforgettable, timeless taste.

The tasting journey begins with an immediate hit of intensely fresh sardine flavor. This maritime note is the epitome of quality seafood - pure, clean, and unadulterated. The olive oil’s presence serves to mellow and enrich this initial burst, adding a velvety texture and a delightful hint of richness. The lemon essence doesn’t compete with the sardines and olive oil. Rather, it subtly highlights their unique characteristics, adding a slight tang that elevates the entire tasting experience.
