Millésime 2025

Sardines à l’huile d’olive vierge extra · Millésime 2025

The cornerstone of the art of preservation.

This product formed the very cornerstone of the art of preservation and served as the benchmark for all other canneries. Created by Charles Rödel’s son, it followed a precise and unmistakable method, ensuring a taste and quality that may be preserved, and savoured, even after ten years.

Rödel vintage sardines balance the savoury flavour of high-quality fish with the subtlety of premium olive oil, carrying whispers of the pristine waters they come from.

Sardines sans arêtes à l’huile d’olive vierge extra

Hand-boned, as once prepared for the royal court.

The finest sardines of each vintage are prepared without delay, on the very day of their catch. Hand-boned sardines were conceived for the royal court, where it was deemed unthinkable that even the most delicate bone might trouble the noble throats of its distinguished guests.

Chica Pica

Sardines Chica Pica à l’huile d’olive vierge extra & aux aromates

A secret spice blend, and a registered trademark of the house.

Chica Pica, “wild girl”: a secret spice blend created for Rödel in the early twentieth century by a Venezuelan friend. A registered trademark and an enduring culinary signature of the house.

Carrot, pickle, chilli, white pepper, clove and laurel give a rich, full flavour with a slightly salty, smoky depth.
